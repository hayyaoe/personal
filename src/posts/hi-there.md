## Aquatic
two months ago I bought a tank, soil, and some plants to make an aquascape. Initially this tank was planned to be a Walstad method tank but it had a rough start with algae bloom, insect infestation, and melting plants on the first month, so I decided to add a proper filtration and cooling to treat those issues. 

I also got a black samurai plakat betta named Noir for the tank, the naming process was fun with suggestions from my friends (they went nuts with it).

The tank is not doing great currently, its caused by hair algae bloom, this is different from the first one and a bit worse, so I cleaned the tank every two to three days and reduced the lighting duration to treat it.
